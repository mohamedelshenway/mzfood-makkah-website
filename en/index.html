About us

MZ FOOD — the taste of home while you're a guest of Makkah

MZ FOOD cooks home-style halal food for guests who value a familiar taste — families, pilgrims and travelers from Russia, Chechnya, the Caucasus and the CIS. We focus on quality, familiar dishes and a fast order — not big promises.
